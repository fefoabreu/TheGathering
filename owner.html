The Weatherlight Manifest

A New Horizon

Every legendary vessel requires an exceptional crew.

For years, Captain Sisay commanded the Weatherlight, navigating the most treacherous and magnificent corners of the multiverse. By her side was Hanna, the Ship's Navigator — a brilliant artificer who understood the heartbeat of the ship's engines better than anyone alive. Together they mapped the unknown, weathered impossible storms, and turned a ragtag crew into a family.

But even the greatest explorers eventually seek a sanctuary. A place where the sunsets touch the mountains, where the tides offer a different kind of magic.

At the top of the Silveira mountains, where the mountain meets the sea, they found TheGathering Silveira.

To ensure this haven operates flawlessly, the old crew has taken over The Commander Deck.

⚓ Hanna · Ship's Navigator

She has descended into the engine room once again. As the architect of our backend she meticulously manages the Commander's Deck — every glowing photo, every curated local card, every seamless connection to the Sonos and Samsung arrays flows through her systems. She is the maker, ensuring the structural integrity of the magic we offer our guests.

🧭 Captain Sisay · At the Helm

She has taken her rightful place at the frontend. Worldly, charismatic and fiercely protective of her passengers, Sisay is our guest-facing concierge. A session at Surfland. The best burger in Garopaba. The projector that refuses to cooperate. She doesn't merely answer — she tutors the exact experience you need, dealing it out like a perfectly timed hand of cards.

Their paths crossed long ago in the depths of the lore, bound by loyalty and a shared mission to guide others safely through the journey. Sisay commanded the legendary flying skyship; Hanna was her hand-picked navigator. They served side by side, travelled the multiverse together, and were closer than crew. Sisay relied on Hanna to map the routes, keep the artifacts aloft and solve the logistical puzzles, while Sisay held the leadership, the strategy and the crew.

Now they have joined TheGathering.
Welcome aboard. The Navigator has charted the course, and the Captain is ready for you.
